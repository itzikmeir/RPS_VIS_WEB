Trust, but Verify: Calibrating Trust with Visual Explanations in AI-Guided Route Planning

Itzik Meir  |  Supervisors: Prof. Tsvi Kuflik & Dr. Joel Lanir

Department of Information Systems, University of Haifa

Abstract This report presents the full statistical analysis for a controlled within-subjects experiment examining how three visualization types — Stacked Bar, Radar Chart, and Heat Map — affect trust calibration and decision-making in an AI-guided, multi-criteria route planning task. The analysis addresses eight research questions: (RQ1) the effect of visualization type on trust calibration score (TCS), response time, workload, confidence, situational awareness, and route accuracy; (RQ2a) a retroactive manipulation check of the Easy/Hard difficulty design and its effect on the same outcomes; (RQ2b) the effect of AI model quality (Optimal vs. Suboptimal) on workload, subjective trust, and behavioral calibration; (RQ2c) learning and practice effects across the session; (RQ2d) whether the visualization effect itself depends on task difficulty (a Difficulty × Visualization interaction, tested with a full two-way repeated-measures ANOVA); (RQ3) the speed–comprehension trade-off; and (RQ4) individual-difference moderators. Three additional validity checks are reported: a presentation-order/counterbalancing check; a confidence-calibration analysis (does self-reported confidence track actual route optimality); and a preference–performance alignment analysis (does each participant's stated favourite visualization actually match the one that gave them the best trust calibration and route accuracy — tested against chance with an exact binomial test). Repeated-measures ANOVA (parametric) and Friedman tests (non-parametric) are reported alongside Kendall's W, Cohen's d, and exact statistical power (computed via the noncentral chi-square distribution rather than a fixed lookup table); a Holm–Bonferroni correction is applied across the RQ1 test family. Results are interpreted with explicit notation of underpowered tests, and every result is accompanied by a plain-language summary alongside the technical statistics.
